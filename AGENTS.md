# Repository guidance

- Preserve the approved zine visual system: near-black, fluorescent lime, warm paper, collage, strong typography, square edges.
- Community tone is open, relaxed, free, and non-transactional. Do not add forced introductions, resource exchange, lead generation, or fictional user activity.
- Dates and venues remain TBD until supplied by the organizer. Label editorial content as editorial and never fabricate news or member posts.
- Never commit real credentials or data exports. Keep only placeholders in `.env.example`.
- Keep changes on `feat/community-migration-and-mvp`; do not merge to `main` or publish a production site without authorization.
- A data-backed discussion feature must fail visibly when its backend is unavailable; never fake successful posting or persistent storage.
